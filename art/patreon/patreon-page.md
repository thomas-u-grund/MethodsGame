## About / description

**The Secret of the Lost Codebook** is a point-and-click adventure game about research methods.

You are a first-year student who has just been told that a research question is not the same as a topic. Across five acts on a slightly unhinged university campus, you sharpen a question, build a theory, collect data, analyse it without fooling yourself, and defend it at the Annual Meeting against a keynote speaker who has turned your small, honest finding into a universal law. Along the way: a rap battle of the founding fathers, a significance casino, an ethics tribunal, a schnitzel hotline, and a great many monkeys.

Every room is illustrated, every line is voiced, and it plays in the browser, on a laptop or a phone.

**Play it for free:** https://lostcodebook.org

**Free for teaching.** You can use the game in your own classroom at no cost. It fits introductory methods courses in sociology and the social sciences: each act covers one stage of a research project (the question, theory, data, evidence, and writing it up). Instructors can register a course at https://lostcodebook.org/teach and let students claim bonus points after each act, with the results emailed to you after each deadline.

**Why a Patreon?** The game is and stays free. But it is a one-person project: I am Thomas U. Grund, a sociologist, and I design, build and run it myself, alongside my academic job, in the evenings (with a lot of help from AI tools for the art, the voices and the code). Developing new material, fixing what players find, and keeping it online (hosting, email, the art and voice tools) takes real time and some money.

If the game was useful to you, your students or your course, support here helps keep it free, online and growing. Thank you.

Questions, ideas, or you are using it in a course? Write to contact@lostcodebook.org.
